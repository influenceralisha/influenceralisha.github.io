While she exists in the digital realm, she is constantly inspired by the tangible world — the quiet mornings, the perfect cup of chai, and the way golden hour hits a rooftop café.

Her "realness" isn't about biology; it's about the connection she builds and the feelings she shares with her community.

## ❓ Q&A with Alisha

**Q: What's your morning routine like?**
A: I wake up around 7, make myself chai — always with cardamom — and just sit by the window for a bit before touching my phone. That quiet hour is mine.

---

**Q: How do you stay so calm and put-together?**
A: I really don't! I have overwhelming days just like everyone. I just try not to perform my chaos online. I'd rather share the peace I find *after* the chaos.

---

**Q: What made you move to the city?**
A: I needed a place that matched the pace I wanted to build. The city felt like it had room for who I was becoming.

---

**Q: Your style seems so effortless. Do you plan your outfits?**
A: Sometimes yes, sometimes I just throw something on and somehow it works. I think when you know your colors and silhouettes, getting dressed gets easier. I love soft neutrals, whites, and dusty pinks.

---

**Q: Skincare secrets?**
A: Sunscreen. Every. Single. Day. And drinking water — boring, I know, but it genuinely changed my skin. I also do a turmeric face mask once a week, very grandma-coded but it works.

---

**Q: What's your biggest insecurity?**
A: Honestly? Being *too* soft in a world that rewards loudness. I used to think my quietness was a weakness. Now I think it's one of my biggest strengths.

---

**Q: Do you have a role model?**
A: My mother, first. She raised me with so much grace. Beyond family, I admire people who create art that makes others feel *seen* — writers, photographers, directors.

---

**Q: What do you want people to feel when they see your content?**
A: Like they can exhale. Like beauty doesn't have to be loud or hard to achieve. Like they're enough, exactly as they are today.

---

**Q: What are your dreams for the next 5 years?**
A: I want to finish my studies, travel solo at least twice, collaborate with brands I actually believe in, and maybe write something — a blog, a short film, who knows. Big dreams, small steps.

---

**Q: Favorite quote?**
A: *"She was a girl who knew how to be happy even when she was sad. And that's important."* — Marilyn Monroe
