About Me

I’m Alisha—a Kolkata-born soul now finding my rhythm in the busy streets of Bengaluru. I grew up on stories, slow rainy afternoons, and the kind of dreams that felt too big for my small room. Today, my content is simply an extension of that: documenting the quiet mornings, the perfect chai, and the outfits that make me feel like myself.

I’m not about the perfectly curated life; I’m about the real one. The one where we’re still figuring things out, one outfit and one coffee shop at a time. I believe beauty is a feeling, not a filter—and I’m here to share the little things that make life feel beautiful, exactly as it is.
